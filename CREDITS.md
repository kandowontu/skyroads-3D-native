# Credits

## Original SkyRoads

SkyRoads was created in Estonia by BlueMoon Software, later known as Bluemoon
Interactive, and released for DOS in 1993. This native project exists because
of the original team's design and technical work.

- Programming: Ahti Heinla, Priit Kasesalu, and Jaan Tallinn
- Graphics and artwork: Kaspar P. Loit
- Music: Ott M. Aaloe
- Intro music: Hasso Brück
- Special thanks: Priit Kull and Paul Varney
- Original publisher: Creative Dimensions

The original DOS distribution identifies the game as copyright Bluemoon
Interactive. No original-game credit is claimed by this native port.

The roster above was cross-checked against the published DOS credits and these
surviving historical sources:

- [BlueMoon's official SkyRoads history and downloads](http://www.bluemoon.ee/history/skyroads/)
- [BlueMoon's SkyRoads music archive](http://www.bluemoon.ee/history/skyroads/music/)
- [BlueMoon team archive](http://www.bluemoon.ee/team/index.html)
- [SkyRoads DOS credits at MobyGames](https://www.mobygames.com/game/1007/skyroads/credits/dos/)
